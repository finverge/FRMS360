## Slide 2: The Problem We Solve

### **Headline: Banks Are Exposed on Three Fronts**

**Payment Fraud**
- ₹5,000+ crore lost annually in Indian banking (payment fraud, structuring, mule networks)
- Traditional controls detect fraud *after* it happens (average 30–60 days post-transaction)
- No real-time defenses against account takeover, device fraud, money-mule rings

**AML/CFT Risk**
- RBI mandates early-warning systems for sanctions, PEP screening, and currency transaction reporting
- Manual processes miss 60%+ of suspicious patterns
- Compliance fines for inadequate AML: ₹1–10 crore per incident

**Credit Risk & Loan Fraud**
- Default rates rising (GNPL at 1.8%+ for select banks)
- Credit deterioration discovered only at default (₹10–50 crore loss per large default)
- Financial statement fraud going undetected for 6–18 months (Nirav Modi, PNB pattern)

**The Gap:** Banks have no unified system to catch payment fraud (real-time) + AML (post-settlement) + credit fraud (quarterly).

## Slide 3: Fraud360 Solution Overview

### **Headline: Three Lanes. One Platform. Complete Coverage.**

**Lane B: Near-Real-Time Screening (Post-Settlement)**
- All 28 fraud signals + AML/CFT screening

**Lane C: Periodic Borrower Assessment (Quarterly)**

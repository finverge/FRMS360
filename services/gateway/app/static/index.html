        Polish narrative
        
      
        Rewrites the narrative below into
        natural prose using an on-premise model — it can only rephrase what is already
        here, never add a fact. Every rewrite is checked automatically before you ever
        see it; a rewrite that adds anything not already evidenced is refused and you
        get the original text instead. Nothing is saved until you choose to prepare the
        return with the text below.

        Record a recovery
        
      
        Every recovery needs a reference behind
        it — a credit advice, claim number or court order. The case's recovered total is
        the sum of these entries, and it is what the board pack reports.
        
      
        
        
        
        
        
        
      
        
      
        
      
        
        
        
      
        
            

    
      
        Issue the pack to the committee
        
      
        Issuing freezes this pack and records who
        received it. It cannot be regenerated afterwards, because the minutes will refer
        to it. Name the recipients — one per line.
        
      
      
      
        
      
        
        
        
      
        
            

    
      
        Record an accountability finding
        
      
        Name the person examined and what was
        concluded about them. "No lapse established" is a finding — record it rather than
        leaving the person out, so the examination shows who was actually looked at.
        
      
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      
        
      
        
      
        
        
        
      
        
            

    
      
        Conclude the examination
        
      
        Once concluded, the findings are final —
        there is no edit or delete path to them. This is what the bank answers with when
        asked how it examined its own staff.
